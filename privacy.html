Privacy notice

Sandy Bay Caravan Park Limited · Company number 06361511

This notice explains what we do with the information you give us through this website. We are a small family-run caravan park and we keep this simple: we only ask for what we need, and we do not sell it to anyone.

What we collect

If you fill in the enquiry form we collect your name, your email address, your telephone number if you give one, and whatever you write in the message. That is all. We do not ask for payment details through this website.

What we use it for

Only to reply to your enquiry and to talk to you about the caravan or the stay you asked about. If you go on to buy or rent from us, we keep your contact details as part of our ordinary business records.

Who sees it

The people who run the park. Your enquiry reaches us by email, so it passes through our email provider on the way. We do not pass your details to other companies for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead anywhere are deleted once they are clearly finished with. Records connected to a sale or a booking are kept for as long as we have to keep business records, which is normally six years.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not track you around the internet.

Our TikTok, Facebook and Google pages

We post photographs of the park and of caravans we have for sale to our own social media accounts. Those posts are made up of our own photographs and descriptions. If you message us on one of those platforms, that platform's own privacy notice applies to the message as well as this one.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us at the address below or telephone 01745 345 054 and we will sort it out. If you are not happy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
